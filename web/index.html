    HOW IT WORKS

    The leak

    Every plane, train and ship within earshot bends the pitch of its engine as it passes: a Doppler curve. Their positions are public (ADS-B, transit feeds, AIS), so someone can predict that curve for every place and time and check which one your audio matches. Matching works on tones far too faint to hear. Mains hum adds a second clue: its frequency drifts in a pattern that power grids log, which timestamps the recording.

    What you keep

    The picture is copied bit for bit, with no re-encoding. Voices and the overall sound stay. The audio becomes mono (multiple mics reveal direction) and the file loses all metadata: location, device, timestamps.

    What cloaking does

      What it can't do

      It reduces location leakage from sound. It doesn't guarantee anything: the picture, the words spoken or your account can still give the place away. So far it's tested on synthetic recordings. Posting later helps too, because every hour multiplies the flights an attacker has to check. Threat model.
